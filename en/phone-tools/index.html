What it does

Presenter remote

Next, previous, start the show and a timer.

Mouse & keyboard

Mouse, keyboard, gyro and joystick from your phone.

Gamepad

A virtual Xbox 360 gamepad for games.

Arabic typing

Type Arabic on your phone straight into the PC.

PC screen

A screenshot or live mirror of your PC on your phone.

Files

Macros & reminders

Macros, reminders, quick notes and push text or links to your phone.
