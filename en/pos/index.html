Fateen POS: the cashier system that never stops

A complete Arabic POS and management system that works 100% without the internet. The internet is only needed for remote support or updates.

Inside the system

Illustrative renders of the interface

Features

Cashier & sales

  • Dine-in, takeaway, delivery and custom order types
  • A different product price per order type
  • Held invoices
  • Returns linked to the original invoice
  • Barcode via scanner or phone camera
  • Coupons and discounts

Inventory & products

  • Full control of price, cost, quantity and expiry
  • Import products from Excel
  • Track shortages and adjustments
  • Favorite products on the cashier screen

Kitchen screen

  • Orders appear in the kitchen the moment they're rung up
  • Status of every order: pending / done

Staff & permissions

  • Roles (manager / cashier)
  • Monthly target, commission and salary per employee
  • Attendance log
  • Every action is tied to who did it

Customers & reports

  • Automatic customer log from delivery
  • Daily and monthly reports and peak hours
  • A separate report for each cashier

Screens & security

  • A second cashier, waiter tablet and kitchen screen on the shop network
  • Runs reliably with up to 3 cashiers
  • Arabic interface, light and dark themes
  • Encrypted passwords
