Privacy Policy

How we handle your data when you contact Fateen or use our website.

What we collect

We only collect what you send us yourself: your name, phone number, business type and message, when you contact us on WhatsApp or fill in the feedback form.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, prepare an offer that fits your business, and improve our services based on customer feedback.

WhatsApp and forms

WhatsApp conversations take place inside WhatsApp and follow its own privacy policy. The feedback form sends your opinion to our team only.

Website analytics

This website does not currently use tracking or analytics tools. If we adopt one in the future, we will say so on this page before enabling it.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. It is used inside the Fateen team only to deliver the service you asked for.

Customer system data

Business data recorded inside the cashier system belongs to the business owner and is stored on their device. Fateen does not access it unless asked for support.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to delete or correct data you sent us, with a simple WhatsApp message.
