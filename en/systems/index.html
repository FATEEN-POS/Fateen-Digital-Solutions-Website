FATEEN ERP

Your real profit on every invoice

A stock and sales management system for equipment and device traders. It runs offline on your shop network, with your data on your device.

Screens from the system

Screenshots from a test version. Customer data is hidden.

How real profit is calculated

Each sale is drawn from the oldest purchase batch, and cost and profit are stored in the invoice at the time of sale.

Batch 1: 10 units × 100Batch 2: 10 units × 120Sell 15 units at 200
Cost (10×100 + 5×120)1,600
Revenue (15×200)3,000
Real profit1,400
Profit if calculated by average cost (110)1,350

A simplified illustrative example; the gap grows with larger numbers.

Features

  • Locked invoices

    No editing. Cancel and return restore stock to the same batches

  • Real profit with FIFO

    Cost and profit are calculated and stored on the invoice at sale time

  • Locked automatic price

    Changing it needs manager permission, with discount and other expenses

  • Receivables and credit limit

    An invoice that exceeds a customer limit warns before saving

  • Serial warranty

    A serial number and warranty period per item, tracked

  • Quotations

    Turn into a sales invoice in one tap

  • Purchases and suppliers

    A statement per supplier; payments go to the oldest open invoice

  • Reports and tax

    Sales, profit, purchases, cash movement and an internal tax report with export (not e-invoicing)

  • Staff and permissions

    The manager sets each staff page; purchase price and profit are manager-only

  • Fateen assistant

    Answers in Arabic from your data

  • WhatsApp and barcode

    A ready invoice or statement message, and barcode-scanner search

  • Daily backup

    On the same device, automatic, keeping the last 30 copies. We recommend copying them to a USB drive or another machine

What is Fateen ERP, and who is it for?

A complete Arabic management system for shops and trading companies: sales, purchases, stock, suppliers, customers, staff and reports. It runs on your shop network without internet and covers the work from the sales invoice to profit and tax reports.

A good fit for

  • Shops and trading companies: devices, equipment, spare parts, wholesale and retail
  • Businesses whose prices change and who write the selling price on the invoice
  • Places where the internet often drops
  • One branch, up to 5 devices on the shop network

Not available yet (planned)

  • Multiple branches
  • Following sales from outside the premises
  • Email alerts
  • Off-device backup
  • Database encryption

Profit for the manager only

Each invoice's profit and margin, with purchase price and profit hidden from staff accounts. Staff sell without seeing your margin.
